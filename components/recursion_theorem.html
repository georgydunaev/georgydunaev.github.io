Recursion Theorem in ZF

Formalized and machine-checked proof of the classical Recursion Theorem following Hrbacek & Jech, published in the Archive of Formal Proofs (Recursion-Addition) ↗. Establishes existence and uniqueness of primitive recursive operations on natural numbers within axiomatic set theory.

∀ A a g. a ∈ A ∧ g ∈ (A × ℕ → A) ⟹ ∃! f ∈ (ℕ → A). f(0) = a ∧ (∀ n ∈ ℕ. f(succ(n)) = g(f(n), n))
theorem nat_rec_exists_unique: assumes "a ∈ A" and "g ∈ A × nat → A" shows "∃!f ∈ nat → A. f ` 0 = a ∧ (∀n ∈ nat. f ` (succ(n)) = g ` ⟨f ` n, n⟩)"
Explicit Side Conditions: 1. a ∈ A (Base value well-typed in codomain set A)
2. g ∈ A × nat → A (Step recursion operator is total and well-defined function)
