Yoneda AI — Some Proofs in Category Theory: The Yoneda Lemma

Overview

yoneda_ai is an AI-assisted formal monograph and mathematical study dedicated to category theory, natural transformations, and constructive proofs of the celebrated Yoneda Lemma and its consequences.

The monograph investigates both covariant and contravariant formulations of representable functors, proving that an object in a locally small category is completely determined up to isomorphism by the network of its morphisms with other objects.

Mathematical Formulation

1. Presheaves and Representable Functors

Let \(\mathcal{C}\) be a locally small category. For any object \(A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})\), the contravariant hom-functor (presheaf): \[ h_A = \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-, A) : \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathbf{Set} \] maps an object \(X\) to the set of morphisms \(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X, A)\), and a morphism \(f : X \to Y\) to pre-composition \(-\circ f : \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(Y, A) \to \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X, A)\).

2. Statement of the Yoneda Lemma

For every object \(A \in \mathcal{C}\) and every presheaf \(F : \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathbf{Set}\), there exists a natural bijection: \[ \Phi_{A, F} : \mathrm{Nat}\big(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-, A), F\big) \overset{\sim}{\longrightarrow} F(A) \] given by evaluating a natural transformation \(\alpha\) at the identity morphism: \[ \Phi_{A, F}(\alpha) = \alpha_A(\mathrm{id}_A) \in F(A). \] The inverse mapping constructs for every element \(u \in F(A)\) a natural transformation \(\alpha^{(u)}\) whose components \(\alpha^{(u)}_X : \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X, A) \to F(X)\) are defined by: \[ \alpha^{(u)}_X(f) = (F(f))(u). \]

3. The Yoneda Embedding

As a direct corollary, setting \(F = h_B = \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-, B)\) yields: \[ \mathrm{Nat}\big(h_A, h_B\big) \cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A, B). \] Hence the Yoneda functor: \[ y : \mathcal{C} \to \mathbf{Set}^{\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}}, \quad A \mapsto h_A \] is fully faithful. In particular, \(h_A \cong h_B\) as presheaves if and only if \(A \cong B\) in \(\mathcal{C}\).

Foundational Context in Morse-Kelley Class Theory

In traditional set theories (ZFC), the collection of all sets or the category of sets \(\mathbf{Set}\) cannot be formed as a set. In the Morse-Kelley (MK) class theory underlying the Obelisk Proof Assistant, a rigorous distinction is maintained:

  • Small Categories: Categories whose collections of objects and morphisms are sets. The category \(\mathbf{Set}\) of small sets is a large category.
  • Large Categories: Categories whose objects and morphisms form proper classes. In Morse-Kelley theory, full impredicative class comprehension guarantees the existence of functor classes and natural transformation classes without introducing inconsistency.
