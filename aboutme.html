  • Proof Assistant "set_theory_prover" (Obelisk):
    A proof assistant for Morse-Kelley (MK) class theory and NBG foundations with a minimal trusted kernel. Semantics is strictly non-computational: the compiler statically typechecks derivations without emitting bytecode. Features verified ordinal arithmetic and formal de Bruijn export.
    [GitHub] (kernel, standard library, and classical deduction engine)
    [link] (project web presentation and dual-pane demonstration)
    [Doc] (monograph on Quotation & Antiquotation, Shift-to-Term principle, and nested stratification)
