Interactive Category Theory Commutative Diagrams

Visualizations of core category-theoretic structures, functorial mappings, and natural transformations formalized in Georgy Dunaev's mathematical foundations research.

1. The Yoneda Lemma: Naturality Square

For any morphism $f: A \to B$, the natural transformation $\alpha: \mathcal{C}(A, -) \Rightarrow F$ commutes with post-composition:

F(f)(α_A(id_A)) = α_B(f ∘ id_A) = α_B(f)

2. Functor Composition Law

Commuting triangle for composition $g \circ f$ mapped through functor $F: \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D}$:

F(g ∘ f) = F(g) ∘ F(f)
